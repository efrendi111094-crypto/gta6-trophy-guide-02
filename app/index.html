    The road to the platinum — 6 stages, all 55 trophies

    One playthrough, no difficulty settings, no mission replay, free roam after the credits. Published roadmaps put the platinum at around 200 hours; the 100% checklist is the longest stretch. Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off your trophy run.

    Disputes, resolved in the open

    • Meditations: 18 or 15? The trophy needs 18 meditations; the 100% checklist needs only 15 of 18. Different counters, both reported — the trophy number is the one that matters for Positive Mental Attitude.
    • Is the Chapter 1 balcony meditation required? PowerPyx and GTABase both say it is unconfirmed: it raises the counter but is not marked as a spot. This guide's call: do it anyway — it costs a minute and removes the risk.
    • Which stranger strands count? Four are confirmed by published guides; the rest are still being confirmed. Strangers & Freaks is marked unconfirmed rather than padded with guesses.

    Missable watch list

    Checked against the official descriptions and the published guides: exactly one trophy is reported as possibly missable. Everything else can be earned after the story in free roam.

    Safe: everything else

    All 12 story mission and chapter trophies are unmissable (mandatory missions). All side quest lines, collectibles, activities and robberies stay available after the credits — including Kenton's treasures, which resume when he texts again later. Nothing on the 100% checklist is missable.
